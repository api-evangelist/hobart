Using a commercial meat slicer to slice whole products like meats and cheeses, as well as vegetables, can bring efficiencies to kitchens when compared to manually cutting these products. Labor, whether in an independent restaurant or K-12 kitchen is at a premium and is better focused on high-value work instead. That includes menu creation, quality control and inventory management — all activities that help streamline processes and improve productivity.
